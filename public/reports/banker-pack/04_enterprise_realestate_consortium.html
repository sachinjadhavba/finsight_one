01Background & Consortium Structure

R████████ Developers & Infra Pvt. Ltd. is a real estate development company headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana, incorporated in 2008. The company undertook a residential township project — Phase 1 consisting of 480 units across 12 towers — in the Sohna Road micro-market of Gurugram. The project commenced construction in FY2018 and had delivered 240 units prior to COVID-19.

The remaining 240 units stalled in March 2020 due to construction halt, labour migration, and a sharp decline in pre-sales collections. The consortium declared the account as NPA in March 2022 after sustained SMA-2 classification through FY21–22. FinsightOne was engaged in April 2025 to structure and represent the OTS proposal.

05RBI Regulatory Framework for OTS

This OTS proposal has been structured in compliance with the applicable RBI regulatory framework for resolution of stressed assets:

  • RBI Circular — Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets (June 7, 2019): Provides the principal framework for resolution plans by lenders, including OTS. Banks are required to implement a resolution plan (RP) within specified timelines.
  • RBI Master Direction on Compromise Settlement and Technical Write-off (August 2023): Explicitly permits OTS for NPA accounts including real estate sector. Haircut below 50% recovery is permissible with Board/SLBC approval.
  • SARFAESI Act 2002: Banks retain enforcement rights as fallback. The OTS offer exceeds expected SARFAESI realisation, making OTS the superior option.
  • IBC 2016 (Insolvency Reference): If OTS is declined and IBC is initiated, CIRP process for real estate typically takes 18–24 months with average recovery of 42–48% (IBBI data FY24) — inferior to proposed OTS recovery of 53%.
  • RERA Act 2016: Homebuyers in stalled projects have priority claims in insolvency (SC judgment in Pioneer Urban Land, 2019). This further reduces bank recovery in liquidation.
Regulatory Conclusion: The proposed OTS is within all applicable RBI parameters. The haircut of 47% is permissible under the August 2023 Master Direction. Board approval at lead bank level is required before formal consortium OTS sanction can be issued.

1. Executive Summary

Investment Thesis

M████████ Agro Foods Pvt. Ltd. is a well-established food processing company based in Nashik, Maharashtra — one of India's premier agro-processing hubs. The company seeks a ₹62 Crore Term Loan for capacity expansion into tomato paste (120 MT/day) and IQF (Individually Quick Frozen) vegetable processing (80 MT/day). The project is technically sound, commercially viable, and backed by strong financials. FinsightOne AI assigns a score of 81/100 (GREEN) — recommend for sanction.

The company has demonstrated consistent revenue growth from ₹89.2 Cr (FY22) to ₹132.6 Cr (FY24) — a CAGR of ~22%. EBITDA margins have improved steadily from 15.0% to 15.8% over the same period, with further expansion projected to 19.1% by FY27P as higher-margin processed products drive mix improvement.

The project leverages Nashik's agro-belt advantage — the region produces 35% of Maharashtra's tomato output — ensuring raw material security at competitive procurement costs. The new processing line will add significant value-add capability and open export channels (Europe, Middle East).

The project is to be set up on the company's existing owned land (4.2 acres) in Nashik, eliminating land acquisition risk. Core machinery is European-origin (Germany/Netherlands) with a 3-year OEM warranty, ensuring technology quality. The IRR of 18.4% and payback period of 4.8 years confirm commercial viability.

The average DSCR of 1.56 over the 7-year repayment period provides comfortable debt service coverage. Security includes equitable mortgage of existing plant (DM value ₹88 Cr), new project assets, personal guarantees of promoters, and corporate guarantee of a group entity.

4. Business Overview
Company Background

M████████ Agro Foods operates a fully integrated food processing facility at Nashik, Maharashtra. The company sources raw materials (tomatoes, onions, vegetables) directly from farmers within a 50 km radius under a contract farming arrangement, ensuring year-round supply at stable prices. The existing plant operates at two shifts with a capacity utilisation of ~72%.

Revenue has grown at a CAGR of 22% over FY22–FY24, driven by expanding FMCG client base (supplying to private label brands) and growing export demand. The company sells under its own brand in domestic retail and as a B2B supplier to large food companies.

Nashik Agro Belt Advantage

Nashik district is the largest tomato-producing region in Maharashtra, accounting for 35% of the state's output. The region also has significant onion, grapes, and vegetable production. Proximity to raw material sources reduces input cost by ~8–10% vs. competitors located in non-agro zones.

PLI Scheme for Food Processing

The company has applied under the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Food Processing (GoI) — the expansion qualifies under the processed fruit/vegetable segment. PLI benefits (estimated ₹3–5 Cr over 6 years) have not been factored into projections — upside risk.

5. Project Description & Technical Feasibility

Project Overview

Installation of a new integrated food processing line comprising: (a) Tomato Paste line — 120 MT/day capacity; (b) IQF (Individually Quick Frozen) Vegetable line — 80 MT/day. The project will be located on the company's existing 4.2-acre owned land in Nashik, with dedicated utility infrastructure (cold storage, effluent treatment, boilers).

Technical Parameters
  • Tomato Paste: Hot-break technology, ASEPTA filling, 28–30° Brix specification
  • IQF Line: Tunnel freezer, blast freezing at -35°C, capacity 80 MT/day
  • Machinery: European-origin (Germany/Netherlands); OEM: ████████ Foodtech GmbH
  • 3-year OEM warranty on core processing equipment
  • Cold storage: 500 MT in-house capacity (built into project)
  • ETP (Effluent Treatment): ZLD compliant — regulatory risk eliminated
  • Plant capacity utilisation assumed at 55% (Year 1) → 75% (Year 3)
Market Demand Assessment
  • India's processed food market: USD 322 Bn (2024), growing at 9.4% CAGR
  • Tomato paste exports: India is the 4th largest global supplier
  • IQF vegetables: Europe import demand growing 12% YoY (EU food safety compliance required — already obtained by company)
  • Company has LOIs from 3 European buyers (Netherlands, Germany, Poland) for IQF supply post-expansion
  • Domestic demand: Private label FMCG segment — confirmed offtake agreements with 2 large buyers

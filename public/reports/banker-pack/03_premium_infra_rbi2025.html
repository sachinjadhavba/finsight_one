The project remains viable (DSCR >1.20x) under Scenarios 1 and 2. Scenario 3 (severe stress — simultaneous traffic shortfall of 30% AND cost overrun of 15%) results in DSCR below covenant — this combination is historically rare for NHAI-awarded projects. The escrow account ensures priority debt service. The lender should require a covenant reset mechanism (DSCR ratchet) if Scenario 3 conditions persist.

Escrow Mechanism — Key Features

All toll revenues are mandatorily routed through the escrow account. No equity distribution is permitted until O&M expenses are fully paid and debt service is fully covered. DSRA must be topped up to 1 quarter before any equity release. The lender has veto rights on any withdrawal from the escrow account outside the defined waterfall.

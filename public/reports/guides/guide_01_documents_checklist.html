What Documents Do Banks
Actually Ask For in 2025?

The exact checklist banks use — and why most loan files get rejected on day one due to missing or incorrect documents. Written by our team of ex-bank credit officers who reviewed thousands of files.

📖 8 min read
👤 For: Business owners applying for any bank loan
✅ Updated for RBI 2025 guidelines
🔑 The Most Important Thing Nobody Tells You

Banks don't reject your loan because your business isn't good enough. Most rejections happen because the documents are wrong, missing, or inconsistent — before a credit officer even evaluates your financials.

The Day-One Rejection Problem

Our team has reviewed hundreds of loan applications. In over 60% of cases where a loan was declined, the core problem was not the business or the financials — it was the document file itself. A mismatch between your GST address and your bank statement address. A financial year gap. A photocopy instead of a certified copy. These are fixable — but only if you know about them before you apply.

🏢 KYC & Entity Documents — Every Loan, Every Bank
Business / Entity Documents
Certificate of Incorporation (COI) Critical
MCA-issued for Pvt. Ltd. / Ltd. companies. Must be original or certified copy. Some banks now accept MCA portal printout with digital signature.
PAN Card — Company & All Directors/Partners Critical
PAN must match CIBIL records exactly. Even a name mismatch (Pvt. Ltd. vs Private Limited) can flag the CIBIL pull.
GST Registration Certificate Critical
Address must match the business address on all other documents. If you have multiple GST registrations (different states), attach all of them.
Memorandum & Articles of Association (MOA & AOA) Critical
For Pvt. Ltd. companies. Certified copy stamped by ROC. Some banks require this to be less than 6 months old (request fresh certified copy from MCA).
Udyam Registration Certificate (MSME) Recommended
Mandatory for accessing MSME-specific loan schemes, lower interest rates under PSL, and CGTMSE guarantee cover. Register free at udyamregistration.gov.in
Board Resolution for Borrowing Critical
For Pvt. Ltd.: Board resolution authorising specific directors to borrow and sign loan documents. Bank provides format — use their format exactly.
Import Export Code (IEC) If Exporter
Required only if export financing (packing credit, PCFC, LC) is being sought.
RBI Note: Under RBI's KYC Master Directions (updated January 2024), banks must accept Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, voter ID, or utility bill as OVD (Officially Valid Document) for address proof. They cannot insist on any one specific document.
📊 Financial Documents — The Most Common Gap
Audited Financial Statements
CA-Audited Balance Sheet + P&L (Last 3 Years) Critical
Must be signed and stamped by a registered Chartered Accountant. All 3 years mandatory — missing even one year is a common rejection trigger. If the business is newer, submit whatever years are available plus a CA certificate.
Provisional Balance Sheet for Current Year Critical
If you're applying mid-year (e.g., September 2025), banks want up-to-date financials. Have your CA prepare provisional accounts for April–August 2025 with a certificate. Without this, banks assume you're hiding current-year stress.
ITR — Company + All Directors/Partners (Last 3 Years) Critical
Must match declared income in Balance Sheet. Common problem: ITR shows turnover of ₹4 Cr but GST returns show ₹8 Cr. Banks use all three (ITR, BS, GST) to cross-check — mismatches trigger red flags.
GST Returns — 12 Months (GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B) Critical
Banks now routinely cross-check GST returns against declared turnover. Late filing or any month with "NIL" filing during active business operations raises questions.
CMA Data (Credit Monitoring Arrangement) Critical for WC Loans
Bank's standard form showing 3 years actual + 2 years projected financials. Must be prepared by your CA in the specific bank-provided format. This is the most commonly missed document for working capital loans. Ask the bank for their CMA format before your CA starts.
Debtors + Creditors Statement (Aging) Critical for CC Loans
For Cash Credit: month-wise aging of outstanding debtors (how long each invoice has been unpaid). Bank uses this to calculate Drawing Power. Old overdue debtors (90+ days) reduce your CC eligibility significantly.
🏦 Bank Account Documents
Banking Statements
Bank Statements — Last 12 Months (All Accounts) Critical
Every current account, savings account, CC account, OD account — all of them. Do not submit statements only from the "good" accounts. Banks run a CIBIL check and will see all accounts anyway. Incomplete submission looks like concealment.
Loan Repayment Track Record (Statement of Existing Loans) Critical
For all existing loans: outstanding balance, EMI amount, due date, payment history. Even one missed EMI in the last 12 months significantly reduces approval probability. If you have missed EMIs, be upfront about the reason.
CIBIL / Credit Report — Company & Promoters Critical
Banks pull this themselves, but it's best to pull your own report first (free once a year at cibil.com) and check for errors. Wrong entries are more common than you think and can take 30–45 days to correct.
⚠️ The Cash Transaction Problem

If your bank statements show large cash withdrawals followed by corresponding cash deposits (even legitimate salary payments), it raises money laundering flags under RBI's AML guidelines. Banks are required to flag and investigate this. If your business legitimately pays wages in cash, have your CA prepare an explanation note in advance — before the bank asks.

🏗️ For Term Loans (Plant / Project Finance)
Project-Specific Documents
Detailed Project Report (DPR) Critical
For term loans above ₹1 Crore — banks require a DPR covering: project background, cost of project, means of finance, revenue projections with assumptions, break-even analysis, DSCR calculation. As of RBI's April 2025 Project Finance Directions, DPR requirements have been tightened for loans above ₹25 Crore.
Quotations from Machinery Suppliers Critical
Banks need to verify the cost of project. Get at least 2 quotations. Proforma invoices (not verbal quotes) are required. For imported machinery, include CIF cost, customs duty, and installation in the quotation breakdown.
Property Documents (for Mortgage) Critical
Title deed, encumbrance certificate (last 30 years), property tax receipts, municipal approved plan, layout plan. Must be free of any prior mortgage. Banks mandate a legal opinion from their empanelled lawyer before disbursement.
Environmental Clearance / Pollution NOC If Applicable
Mandatory for manufacturing industries that require a Consent to Establish / Consent to Operate from the State Pollution Control Board. Banks will not disburse without this for applicable industries.
✅ The 5 Documents That Most Often Derail Applications
  • Provisional accounts for the current year — 70% of applicants don't have this and it makes the file look outdated
  • CMA Data in bank's own format — clients often get CMA Data prepared generically; each bank has its own format
  • Promoter's personal ITR matching company income — if the promoter draws no declared salary, it's a red flag
  • Clean debtors aging — no 90+ day outstanding — old uncollected invoices directly reduce CC eligibility
  • Consistent address across all documents — GST, bank, ITR, property documents must all show the same address
💡 Pro Tip from Ex-Bankers

Before submitting any application, do a self-audit: read your own file as if you're a suspicious credit officer. If you find a mismatch between any two documents — fix it before submitting. The bank will find it. Better it comes from you with an explanation than from them with a doubt.

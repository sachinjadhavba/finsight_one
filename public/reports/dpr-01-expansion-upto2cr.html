Rajesh Engineering Works is a proprietorship established in 2009, engaged in precision CNC machining of auto components — specifically turned parts, housings, and sub-assemblies for Tier-1 automotive suppliers in the Pune–Chakan–Talegaon belt. The unit operates from a 4,800 sq ft owned factory at Plot C-47, MIDC Bhosari, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune – 411026.

The business has grown steadily — net turnover crossed ₹1.10 Crore in FY2023–24, with a net profit margin of 16%. Existing machinery is running at 88% utilisation, and the business has confirmed purchase orders from two Tier-1 customers that it cannot fully service with current capacity. This project adds two Ace Micromatic CNC Turning Centers to expand monthly machining capacity from 28,000 to 48,000 components.

The Opportunity in One Line: Confirmed orders worth ₹68 lakhs/year are sitting unfulfilled due to lack of capacity. This expansion directly captures that business.

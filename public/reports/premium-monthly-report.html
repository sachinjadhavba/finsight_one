Your narrative should lead with: "We are requesting enhancement basis 18% turnover growth year-on-year, improved DSCR, and our plan to route additional export billing through the SBI account." Routing more transactions through SBI is the single most effective thing you can say to a PSU banker.
What NOT to Volunteer in the Meeting
Do not mention that you also have an ICICI account with a ₹60L OD unless asked directly. Do not bring up the personal loan you took in January. Do not say "we need funds for expansion" without being specific — vague expansion stories weaken a credit case. Instead say: "We need the additional limit to fund the 45-day gap between invoice raising and payment collection from our top 3 clients."
🗣️ Banker Meeting Script
June 2026 Enhancement Meeting · SBI
Opening (first 90 seconds): "Good morning. We have been banking with SBI for 6 years. Our turnover has grown 18% this year to ₹3.8Cr. We are here to request a CC enhancement from ₹1.2Cr to ₹1.5Cr to support the increase in our receivables cycle."
When banker asks: "Why do you need more limit now?"
Say: "Our three largest clients — Hindustan Unilever, Tata Consumer, and Reliance Retail — have moved to 45-day payment terms. Our working capital gap has increased proportionally. The enhancement request is directly linked to the billing growth, not new expenses."
Do NOT say: "Business is slow and we need cash."
When banker asks: "Your utilisation was high in March?"
Say: "March was our peak billing month — we had back-to-back dispatches. The account normalised within 8 days. If you see our average utilisation over 6 months it is 64%, which reflects disciplined usage."
When banker asks: "What security can you offer?"
Say: "We are offering the existing hypothecation of book debts and stock. If required, we can add a personal guarantee. We believe the strength of our financial ratios — DSCR 1.68, zero cheque returns, clean ITRs — makes this an unsecured-equivalent risk for the bank."
Closing line (most effective):
"We route all our primary business through SBI. We are asking for this enhancement because we want to grow this relationship further — not split limits across banks."
❓ Pre-Meeting Mock Q&A
10 likely questions from your credit manager
Q1. Your Net Profit margin seems thin at 4.2% — how will you service enhanced debt?
A: Our business model runs on thin margins at the gross level but our DSCR of 1.68 shows comfortable debt servicing capacity. The CC is working capital, not term debt — it gets repaid from collections within 30–45 days, not from profit margins.
💡 Tip: Have your latest P&L in hand. Don't get defensive — acknowledge the margin and redirect to cash flow.
Q2. You have an OD with ICICI also. Why do you need limit at SBI?
A: The ICICI OD is used for a specific set of clients who prefer ICICI collections. Our SBI account handles the bulk of our business — 68% of our total collections come into this account. The two limits serve different purposes and different customer segments.
💡 Tip: Do not say the ICICI limit is insufficient. Frame it as deliberate account management.
Q3. You haven't taken any term loan with us. Why are you loyal to us for CC only?
A: We are fully open to consolidating our banking with SBI. Once this CC enhancement is in place and we see the limit working well, our next step would be to discuss a term loan for our warehouse expansion with SBI. We would prefer a single banking relationship.
💡 Tip: This is an excellent question — treat it as an opening, not a trap.
Q4. Your debtors are ₹42L — can you share the debtor list?
A: Yes, I have the debtor ageing statement with me. Our top 5 debtors account for 78% of the receivables — all are large corporates with regular payment history. The ageing shows 91% of debtors within 60 days.
💡 Tip: Always bring a debtor ageing statement to this meeting. Amit has prepared one for you.
Q5. What is the purpose of enhancement — stock or debtors?
A: Primarily debtors. With the growth in business, our receivables cycle has extended from 35 to 48 days. The additional ₹60L will cover the gap between invoice raise and collection for 2 additional large clients we onboarded in Q3.
💡 Tip: Always tie the enhancement ask to a specific business purpose. Vague answers delay approvals.

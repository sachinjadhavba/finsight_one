Section 09
Break-Even — When the Project Covers Its Costs
What break-even means in simple terms
Break-even is the point at which the project's revenue equals its total costs — it is neither making a profit nor a loss. A lower break-even capacity means the project can stay viable even when demand is slow. We calculate this from the actual cost structure.

Savitri Agro Processing Private Limited (proposed company, incorporation in process) is being promoted by Mr. Vishwanath Ramrao Shinde and Mrs. Sunanda Vishwanath Shinde of Latur, Maharashtra. The company proposes to set up a mechanised dal milling and pulse cleaning/grading unit with a processing capacity of 15 MT per day of raw pulses (tur, chana, moong, urad).

Latur district is one of India's largest pulse-growing regions — Maharashtra contributes ~20% of India's total pulse production, with Latur, Osmanabad, and Nanded being the primary cultivation belt. Despite being at the heart of raw material supply, the region has very limited value-added processing capacity. Most raw pulses travel 200–400 km to processors in Solapur, Hyderabad, or Nagpur, only to return as packaged dal at higher cost. This project fills that gap.

The Business Logic: Buy raw tur/chana at farm gate (₹62–68/kg). Process into clean, graded, packaged dal. Sell to wholesalers, ration shops, and direct to institutions at ₹85–92/kg. Value addition of ₹20–25/kg on 15 MT/day = ₹3,000–3,750 value addition per day.

Risk Analysis — Being Transparent About Challenges
Risks the business faces and how each is mitigated
High
Raw Material Price Volatility: Pulse prices swing 20–40% in a season based on monsoon. Mitigation: Promoter has 18 years of commodity trading experience — knows when to buy forward. Seasonal price drop (post-harvest) is used to build inventory. Raw material sourced directly from farmers at pre-agreed prices through FPO network.
Medium
Ramp-Up Risk: Building a customer base from zero takes time. Mitigation: Promoter already has wholesale customer relationships through Shinde Agri Traders; government procurement pipeline via NAFED empanelment provides assured offtake from Month 10.
Medium
Civil Construction Delay: Factory building may take longer than 5 months. Mitigation: 12-month moratorium requested (standard for greenfield MSME); contractor shortlisted; design finalised. 2-month buffer built into schedule.
Low
Competition: Larger processors from Solapur entering Latur market. Mitigation: Location advantage (lower logistics cost), fresher product (processed closer to source), and government/institutional buyer preference for local procurement.
Low
DSCR Shortfall in Years 1–3: Cash flow insufficient to service debt in early years. Mitigation: Promoter's existing business (Shinde Agri Traders) generates ₹28L+ annual surplus to bridge gap; personal assets as collateral provide security buffer.
Low
Regulatory / FSSAI: Delay in food processing licence. Mitigation: FSSAI application to be submitted 3 months before trial production; FSSAI processing takes 30–60 days typically. Well within schedule.

FinsightOne's Advisory Assessment
Overall view on the project's viability and fundability
ADVISORY SUMMARY — SAVITRI AGRO PROCESSING PVT LTD

This is a well-conceived greenfield project built on genuine market insight. The promoter knows the pulse trade from the inside — he has been buying and selling the same raw material for 18 years that the factory will now process. That is the ideal profile for a greenfield agro-processing investment.

The early-year DSCR will require bridging from the promoter's existing business — this is typical and expected for greenfield agro-processing. What banks should weigh carefully: security coverage of 163%, a promoter with proven commodity market experience and government procurement access, and a project in a region severely undersupplied with dal milling capacity.

We assess this as a viable greenfield proposal — best suited for Bank of Maharashtra (Agro Plus scheme), SBI (PMEGP/MSME), or NABARD-refinanced lending under agro-infrastructure. A 12-month moratorium and Government of India PMFME subsidy of 35% on machinery (if applied) would significantly improve Year 1–2 economics.

Subsidy Opportunity: This project may be eligible for the PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME) scheme — credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% of eligible project cost (max ₹10 lakh per unit) for new food processing units. FinsightOne will assist in applying for this subsidy alongside the loan.

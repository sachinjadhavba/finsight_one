05 GAPS & WEAKNESSES — IDENTIFIED, CONTEXTUALISED, MITIGATED
GAP 1 — FY25 Net Loss of Rs. 18 Lakhs
High Priority
OBSERVATION

The company reported a net loss of Rs. 18 Lakhs in FY25 — the first loss year in its operating history. This is a red flag in any credit assessment and will be the first query from any banker reviewing this file.

CONTEXT

The loss is entirely attributable to two non-recurring, capex-related charges: (1) Rs. 96 Lakhs incremental depreciation on the new 800-ton press line (front-loaded under WDV method, normalises to Rs. 62L from FY26 onwards); (2) Rs. 45 Lakhs bridge finance interest during the 7-month installation period (fully repaid, not a recurring cost). Operating EBITDA before new depreciation was Rs. 354 Lakhs — the highest in 3 years. Cash accruals were Rs. 130 Lakhs — positive.

MITIGANT

Documented evidence: (1) Fixed asset register showing new press line capitalisation date and WDV depreciation schedule — confirms the Rs. 96L charge is a one-time effect. (2) Bridge finance closure letter — Rs. 45L interest was paid and the facility closed in October 2024. (3) FY26 provisional P&L (6 months) showing PAT of Rs. 22.4 Lakhs — full-year PAT projected at Rs. 36 Lakhs. (4) New press line client order — confirming incremental revenue from the asset that caused the loss.

FINSIGHTONE VIEW

Not a credit concern when properly documented. A loss year caused by front-loaded depreciation on a revenue-generating asset is fundamentally different from an operational loss. The test is: are cash flows positive? Yes — Rs. 130L. Is the loss recurring? No — both drivers are gone in FY26. Residual risk: LOW with documentation enclosed.

GAP 2 — TOL/TNW at 2.84x (Post-Proposed TL)
Medium Priority
OBSERVATION

Post-proposed TL of Rs. 2.20 Crore, TOL/TNW rises to approximately 2.84x. For some lenders with stricter internal norms (2.5x cap), this may trigger a query or require explicit risk committee approval.

CONTEXT

The elevated TOL/TNW is a direct consequence of the new press line investment — the same investment that caused the FY25 loss year. The asset is now operational and contributing revenue. Net worth was temporarily impacted by the FY25 loss (Rs. 18L deduction from reserves). With FY26 projected PAT of Rs. 36L + reduced depreciation, net worth is recovering. Auto component manufacturers with new machinery investments routinely show elevated leverage in Year 1 post-capex.

MITIGANT

TOL/TNW is at 4.00x maximum in most lender policies — 2.84x is well within norm. Declining trajectory: FY26 est. 2.42x, FY27 est. 2.08x as net worth recovers and TL is repaid. No dividend policy — 100% profit retention ensures rapid net worth recovery. Personal net worth of directors (Rs. 4.84 Cr combined) provides additional comfort beyond company leverage.

FINSIGHTONE VIEW

Acceptable — declining trend, within 4x norm. The trajectory matters more than the point-in-time level. 2.84x → 2.42x → 2.08x over 24 months shows the leverage is temporary. Residual risk: LOW — declining.

GAP 3 — OEM Client Concentration (68% in Top 2 Clients)
Medium Priority
OBSERVATION

Two OEM clients — a Tier-1 auto supplier and a major two-wheeler component maker — account for 68% of revenue. Loss of either client would materially impact the borrower's revenue and debt service capacity.

CONTEXT

Auto component supply relationships are long-duration and sticky — OEM customers invest in vendor development, tooling, and qualification processes that take 12–18 months and represent significant sunk costs. Once qualified, suppliers are rarely replaced without a quality failure or a major price divergence. Both key clients have been with the borrower for 8+ years.

MITIGANT

(1) Both clients have multi-year supply agreements with annual renewal clauses — copies enclosed. (2) New press line capacity has attracted 3 new Tier-1 inquiries — 2 in qualification process currently. (3) Single client loss scenario stress test: if Client 1 (38% revenue) is lost, DSCR on remaining revenue is 1.42x — still above minimum 1.0x. Business survives a single client loss.

FINSIGHTONE VIEW

Manageable — mitigated by relationship stickiness and active diversification. OEM supply relationships of 8+ years are significantly more durable than comparable relationships in trading or service businesses. The new press line's capacity is actively attracting new clients. Residual risk: MEDIUM — watch item for next renewal.

Executive Summary
Credit Submission Overview
Term Loan of Rs.1.80 Crore for Machinery Acquisition — Steel Fabrication Unit, Coimbatore
Srinivas Steel Works, a proprietorship concern engaged in structural steel fabrication and supply, is seeking a Term Loan of Rs.1.80 Crore for the acquisition of a CNC plasma cutting machine and hydraulic press brake to expand production capacity from the current 180 MT/month to 320 MT/month. The unit has been in operations for 9 years at its registered premises in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.
The borrower presents a clean credit bureau profile (CIBIL 741), consistent ITR-declared income of Rs.68L in FY25, and banking turnover of Rs.1.42Cr for the same period — indicating sound banking discipline. Existing debt obligations are limited to a vehicle loan (outstanding Rs.4.8L) with impeccable repayment history. The proposed loan is fully backed by primary hypothecation of funded machinery and supported by collateral in the form of a residential property at an LTV of 58%.
The expansion is demand-led — the borrower has confirmed orders from 3 infrastructure contractors amounting to Rs.2.8Cr over the next 12 months, copies of which are enclosed in the document dossier. Post-expansion DSCR is projected at 2.1x, providing comfortable debt servicing headroom.

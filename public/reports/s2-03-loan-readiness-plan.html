Banking — Build the inflow trail lenders will see
Turnover routing, bounce elimination, statement cleanup
WEEK 3–4
Route All Collections Through Bank
You
→
Deposit all pending cash collections immediately. For pharma distribution, distributor payments often come in cash — these must hit your current account same day, not end of week.
→
Switch all customer payment instructions to NEFT / RTGS / UPI to current account. Share new instructions with your top 10 clients this week.
✓
Ensure monthly credits average Rs.15L+ for at least 2 consecutive months before applying. Lenders typically look at last 3–6 months of bank statement.
WEEK 4–5
Statement Hygiene & Bounce Check
You
→
Eliminate all cheque bounces. Even 1 outward bounce in the last 3 months will be flagged. Set up auto-credit for loan EMI accounts if any.
→
Avoid large unexplained cash withdrawals in the review period. Every big debit without a matching business purpose weakens the statement narrative.
✓
Maintain EOD balance of Rs.50,000+ on most days. Consistently low balances signal liquidity stress to lender analysts reviewing statements.

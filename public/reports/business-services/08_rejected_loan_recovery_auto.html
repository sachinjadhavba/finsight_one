02 Bank's Rejection Grounds — Root Cause Analysis

The bank's rejection communication (received February 14, 2025) cited three primary grounds. Our team has reviewed the original submission in detail and identified the root cause behind each rejection ground.

Rejection Ground 1: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Below Minimum

Bank's Position: Average DSCR of 1.08x over the loan tenure is below the bank's internal benchmark of 1.25x minimum.

FinsightOne Finding: The borrower's CA had computed depreciation on Written Down Value (WDV) method in projections, but the bank's credit officer recalculated using Straight Line Method (SLM) — creating a ₹18.4 lakh discrepancy in annual cash flow. Correcting for this alone restores DSCR to 1.22x. Additionally, a ₹12 lakh annual maintenance contract (AMC) from a new customer (contract signed December 2024) was not factored into projections.

Rejection Ground 2: Collateral Shortfall — Security Coverage Below 100% of Loan

Bank's Position: Primary security (hypothecation of plant & machinery) + mortgage of industrial plot valued at ₹6.20 Crore covers only 73% of ₹8.50 Crore requested.

FinsightOne Finding: The bank's empanelled valuer used a conservative Forced Sale Value (FSV) adjustment of 40% on the industrial plot. Chakan MIDC plots are classified as "Developed Industrial Area" under Maharashtra MIDC Act — RBI's Master Circular on Loans & Advances permits FSV adjustment of max 25% for developed industrial zones (Ref: RBI/2015-16/100, Para 4.2.3). Correct FSV brings collateral coverage to 94%. The remaining gap of ₹54 lakh can be bridged via a personal guarantee by the promoter (immovable property, Pune).

Rejection Ground 3: Stressed Net Worth — Declining Trend Over 3 Years

Bank's Position: Net worth declined from ₹3.42 Crore (FY22) to ₹2.91 Crore (FY24) indicating stress on equity base.

FinsightOne Finding: The decline is entirely attributable to large one-time losses in FY23 (post-Covid supply chain disruption — ₹47 lakh write-off on raw material) and FY24 forex losses (₹28 lakh on steel import contracts). These are non-recurring. FY25 provisional net worth stands at ₹3.18 Crore. Promoter has confirmed infusion of ₹50 lakh fresh equity in Q1 FY26 (post-loan sanction), bringing net worth above FY22 levels. A notarised undertaking to this effect is available.

RBI Guideline Reference: RBI Master Circular on Loans & Advances — Collateral Security — RBI/2015-16/100, DBOD.No.Dir.BC.10/13.03.00/2015-16 — Para 4.2.3: "FSV of land and building located in developed urban/industrial areas shall not be discounted by more than 25%." Banks must provide written communication of rejection with specific reasons — RBI Fair Practices Code for Lenders.

01Executive Snapshot

G████████ Hospitality Pvt. Ltd. is a premium boutique hospitality company operating two heritage-themed hotels in North Goa. Established in 2012 by a management team with over two decades of combined hospitality experience, the company has built a strong reputation in the luxury boutique segment, commanding an Average Daily Rate (ADR) of ₹8,200 — significantly above the Goa market average of ₹6,400.

The company seeks ₹28 Crore in structured bank financing to renovate and expand Hotel 2 (adding 7 rooms, a rooftop restaurant, and a swimming pool) and to maintain operational working capital through the construction period. FinsightOne has evaluated this as a strong credit case with DSCR well above 1.4x across all projection years.

10Why This is a Strong Credit — FinsightOne Analysis
1
Debt-free company with proven P&L trajectory: The borrower is entering its first bank credit relationship entirely debt-free. FY22–FY24 shows a consistent and accelerating revenue and margin story — Revenue CAGR 30%, EBITDA margin expanded from 31% to 38.8%. No prior defaults, no bureau concerns. This is a textbook first-time credit candidate.
2
Goa's boutique hospitality sector is structurally under-supplied: Planning regulations in heritage micro-markets (Anjuna, Candolim) limit new supply, creating a structural tailwind for existing quality operators. The company's ADR premium of 28% over the market validates product-market fit and pricing power that will compound as tourism volumes grow.
3
Conservative capex with validated demand: The ₹22 Crore hard capex is grounded in independently verifiable market benchmarks (room renovation cost ₹8–10L/room; rooftop restaurant fit-out ₹4–5 Cr). The revenue uplift model is conservative — post-renovation ADR of ₹11,200 for new suites is below comparable properties already operating in Candolim.
4
DSCR comfort with stress buffer: Average DSCR of 1.68x across FY26–FY28, with stressed DSCR of 1.43x (at 15% revenue haircut) remaining above the 1.25x bank threshold. This gives the lending bank meaningful cushion against occupancy or ADR underperformance during the ramp-up period.
5
Experienced management with proven asset management: The promoter team's background — former Oberoi senior leadership, CA-qualified finance director — is the strongest risk mitigant in this sector. The company has grown occupancy from 58% to 74% over 2 years through direct booking development and revenue management, demonstrating active management alpha well above sector norms.

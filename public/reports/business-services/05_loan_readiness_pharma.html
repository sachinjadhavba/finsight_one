02Critical Issues — Red Flags to Fix First

The following 5 issues must be resolved before approaching any scheduled commercial bank. These are non-negotiable thresholds in the bank credit assessment process:

Critical Issue 1 — CIBIL Score

Director 1 CIBIL: 641 — Must Cross 700

Most PSU and private banks require a minimum director CIBIL of 700 for term loans above ₹1 Crore. At 641, automatic screening rejection is triggered. Timeline to cross 700: 6–8 months with disciplined credit behaviour (zero CC defaults, reduced utilisation, no fresh enquiries).

Critical Issue 2 — CC Overutilisation

Must Maintain CC Utilisation Below 80% for 6 Consecutive Months

3 instances of CC overutilisation in the past 12 months signal cash flow stress to bank algorithms. The borrower must demonstrate consistent utilisation below 80% for minimum 6 months in all bureau records before applying.

Critical Issue 3 — NBFC Debt

Repay ₹1.8 Crore NBFC Debt Before Application

Two NBFC loans at 24% interest rate are visible in CIBIL. These increase TOL/TNW ratio adversely and signal inability to access cheaper bank credit. Repayment of both before application is essential — it also improves CIBIL score by 15–20 points.

Critical Issue 4 — GST & ITR Mismatch

GST Q3 FY24 Pending; ITR-6 vs Bank Statement Gap of ₹2.1 Crore

Pending GST filing creates compliance flag. ₹2.1 Crore discrepancy between ITR-6 and bank statement will be flagged in CAM analysis — bank credit officer cannot reconcile without a formal rectification note. File GST return and provide reconciliation immediately.

Critical Issue 5 — DPR Quality: The WHO-GMP plant upgrade project lacks a professionally prepared Detailed Project Report (DPR). For term loans above ₹1 Crore, banks require a DPR with technical feasibility, cost estimates, implementation schedule, and financial projections. FinsightOne's DPR advisory service (₹25,000 add-on) is strongly recommended and required before submission.
